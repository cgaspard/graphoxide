Recommended hybrid

A beautiful map for orientation.
A precise lens for action.

Use Constellation as the global canvas because it preserves Graphoxide’s current renderer shape, scales deliberately, and best delivers the requested visual lift. Make Investigation Lens a focused mode entered from a selected node. Borrow Semantic Atlas’s evidence filters and semantic encodings across both.

This is not an aesthetic winner-take-all choice. It separates two different jobs—discovering the system and answering a code question—while keeping one graph contract, selection, filter state, and source action.

Semantic Atlas

✓ Pieces that combine coherently

  • One deterministic community layout can feed the overview and preserve location between visits.
  • Atlas confidence patterns and artifact shapes strengthen both visual modes without changing topology.
  • A selected Constellation node can open the Lens with the same ID, filters, and reveal/explain actions.
  • Constellation edge budgets and Lens hop bounds provide complementary, explicit density limits.
  • Keyboard commands, announcements, and reduced-motion behavior can share one interaction contract.

× Pieces that should stay separate

  • Do not mix Atlas lanes and Constellation clusters in one geometry; they teach incompatible spatial models.
  • Do not overlay Lens cards across the complete graph; its clarity comes from hiding unrelated topology.
  • Do not merge the cream editorial and dark operational palettes; adapt both modes to shared VS Code tokens.
  • Do not ship the mock risk score or source preview as graph truth until their provenance is explicit.
  • Do not rely on glow or color alone; retain Atlas/Lens shapes, labels, and line patterns.
Low-risk next step

Prototype one production slice before choosing the full skin.

Port Constellation’s deterministic layout, focus fading, and edge budgets behind the existing Graphoxide data and message bridge. Then test a selected-node Lens on real repositories at the 750-node cap. Keep the two modes independently removable until usability and performance numbers justify the combined direction.
